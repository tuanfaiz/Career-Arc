# CareerArc — Mentor Meeting Prep

A cheat sheet for explaining CareerArc, the SDG page, and the Ministry page.
Keep this open (or printed) during the meeting.

---

## Part 1 — How to explain CareerArc

Don't list features. Tell a **story around one idea**, then click through to prove it.

### The 20-second opener
> "CareerArc is a *Career Operating System* for Malaysia. Most job platforms only help you
> *apply*. Ours connects **three people who never talk to each other today** — the job seeker,
> the employer, and the university — around **one single number: the Career Readiness Score.**"

### The problem (say it in plain words)
> "A fresh graduate sends 80 applications and gets ghosted. They don't know why. The employer
> can't tell who's actually good. And the university only finds out their students couldn't get
> jobs *after* they graduate — too late to help. It's not a skills problem, it's an
> **information problem.** Three groups, all blind."

### The big idea (emphasise this)
> "We give every student one **Career Readiness Score** out of 100, built from 5 things: resume,
> ATS, skill match, portfolio, and job activity. The clever part is the **same score is read
> three ways**:
> - The **student** sees it as *'how ready am I, what do I fix next?'*
> - The **employer** sees it as a *hiring signal.*
> - The **university** sees it as a *warning light* to help students early.
>
> One number. Three points of view. That's what makes it one connected system, not three
> separate tools."

### The live demo (click, don't talk) — the golden path
1. **Login → one-click "Candidate"** — "No password, judges get straight in."
2. **Onboarding → Fresh Graduate** — "Watch the readiness score climb as they fill each step."
3. **Dashboard** — "Their score, plus an AI tip on the single best thing to fix next."
4. **A job → View & Compare** — "Shows which skills they have/are missing, and a verdict: apply or not."
   → click **Apply** → **My Applications** — "They can track every application's status."
5. **Switch to Employer** → click an applicant — "Same student; employer now sees score, personality, full resume."
6. **Switch to University** — "Same scores; university sees which students are at risk and what help to give."
7. **End on Impact & SDG** — "And it all ties to the UN Global Goals."

### Likely mentor questions + answers
- **"Is the data real?"** → "Simulated dummy data for the prototype — the hackathon rules ask
  for that. The logic and workflow are real; only the numbers are seeded."
- **"Is the AI real?"** → "We simulate the AI responses to show the thinking, exactly as the
  brief allows. Real AI is a bonus for the next stage."
- **"How is this different from JobStreet / LinkedIn?"** → "They connect *candidate to
  employer*. We add the **university** as a third player and give everyone **one shared score** —
  nobody else does the early-intervention angle."

### Questions to ask your mentor (mentors love being asked)
- "Which user should be the star of our final pitch — candidate, employer, or university?"
- "Is the 'one score, three views' idea clear, or should we make it more obvious?"
- "Which SDG angle is strongest for the judges?"

## Part 2 — The SDG page, explained simply

**What is an SDG?**
In 2015, almost every country in the world made a **"to-do list to fix the planet by 2030."**
That list has **17 goals** — things like *no poverty, good schools, clean water, decent jobs.*
Each goal has a number. They're called the **UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** — think
of them as the **world's homework checklist.**

**What our SDG page does**
It's CareerArc raising its hand and saying: *"Out of the world's 17 goals, we help with THREE."*
It's our **report card for doing good.**

| Goal | Real name | What it means (simple) | How we help |
|------|-----------|------------------------|-------------|
| **SDG 4** | Quality Education | Good schools that teach useful stuff | We show universities which skills students lack, so they teach the right things |
| **SDG 8** | Decent Work | Fair jobs where people are treated well | We show fair salary info and stop employers ghosting people |
| **SDG 17** | Partnerships | Everyone working together | We put students, employers and universities on ONE platform |

- **Top numbers** (312K, +9pts, −28%, −61%) = our *"look how much good we did"* scoreboard.
- **Bottom row** (Candidates, Employers, Universities, Ministry) = *"everyone wins, not just one side."*

**Why this page exists:** judges care a lot about **real impact and sustainability**, not just
features. This page proves *"we're not just an app — we help the country and match the world's
goals."*

## Part 3 — The Ministry page, explained simply

**Who is "the Ministry"?**
The **government office in charge of ALL universities in Malaysia** (real name: MOHE — Ministry
of Higher Education). Imagine a **principal** — but for *every university in the whole country.*

**Their problem:** there are **312,000 fresh graduates every year.** They can't look at students
one by one. They need a **big screen showing the health of the whole country's graduates at a
glance** — like a car dashboard, but for *"how are our graduates doing?"*

**What's on the screen:**
- **Top 4 numbers** — the headlines: 312,000 graduates, 84% got jobs, avg 3.8 months to find one, 50 universities tracked.
- **Employability by Field** — which subjects get jobs easily vs struggle (Computing 89% vs Arts 73%). Tells the Minister *where to send help.*
- **National Employment Trend** — is it getting better or worse? Ours climbs 75% → 84% = improving.
- **Where Graduates Are Hired** — which industries hire most (Technology biggest at 28%).
- **SDG box** — connects it back to the world's goals.

**One-sentence version:**
> "The Ministry page is the **bird's-eye view** — the government sees the whole country's graduate
> situation on one screen, spots which subjects are in trouble, and decides where to send help."

**Why it's powerful:** it proves CareerArc isn't just for one student — the *same readiness data*
scales all the way up to national policy.

## The 3 things to remember
1. **One score, three views** — the heart of the whole product.
2. **Impact over features** — that's what judges reward.
3. **Show, don't tell** — always click through the live demo.
