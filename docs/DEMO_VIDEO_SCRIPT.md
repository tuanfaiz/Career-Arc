# CareerArc — Demo Video Script
### Talentbank Tech Hackathon 2026 · Target length ≈ 5 minutes

**Before recording:** open the site, press F12 → Application → Local Storage → **Clear**, so onboarding starts fresh. Hide bookmarks bar. Have the SIFU answers below copied somewhere to paste — don't type them live on camera.

---

## 0:00 – 0:25 · OPENING (the problem)

**SHOW:** Landing page, scroll slowly.

> "Every year Malaysia produces over three hundred thousand fresh graduates. Many of them send eighty applications and hear nothing back.
>
> But the problem starts much earlier — at seventeen, choosing a course with almost no idea where it actually leads.
>
> This is CareerArc. One platform connecting three groups who today never talk to each other: candidates, employers, and universities."

---

# PART 1 — CANDIDATE *(our main focus)*

## 0:25 – 1:00 · Course Advisor

**SHOW:** Click **Candidate** → sidebar → **Course Advisor**. Pick a few interests and subjects, show the 3 results.

> "We start before university. A seventeen-year-old doesn't know what to study. Course Advisor asks three things — what you enjoy, what you're good at, and how you work.
>
> But look at what each result actually shows. Not just a course name — where it leads, whether demand is rising or cooling, an honest note on whether it will still matter in ten years…
>
> …and a route that doesn't need a degree at all. Because a lot of Gen Alpha are telling us they're not going to university."

## 1:00 – 1:30 · Career Path

**SHOW:** **Career Path**. Click between Path A / B / C. Scroll to the scenarios and Future-Proof Check.

> "Once you've chosen, Career Path shows what your next four years actually look like — year by year, what to *do*, not just what to become.
>
> We deliberately don't show a forty-year plan. The world changes too fast for that. Anything beyond four years we label as *scenarios*, not predictions.
>
> And this — the future-proof check — shows which of your skills last, and which ones AI is already replacing."

## 1:30 – 2:00 · Onboarding + Readiness Score ⭐ *(added — this is the spine)*

**SHOW:** **Onboarding**. Pick *Fresh Graduate*, click through the steps. **Let the score visibly climb.**

> "Now you've graduated. Onboarding builds your profile — and watch the number on the right.
>
> That's your Career Readiness Score, out of a hundred. Resume, ATS, skill match, portfolio, and how actively you're applying.
>
> Remember this number. It's the single most important thing in CareerArc, and I'll show you why shortly."

## 2:00 – 2:20 · Story Video

**SHOW:** **My Story Video**. Show the four prompts. (Record a few seconds, or just show the interface.)

> "But a score isn't a person. So every candidate records a guided sixty-second Story Video — four prompts: who you are, what problem interests you, what you're doing about it, and what you're looking for.
>
> This is what a resume can't show."

## 2:20 – 3:05 · SIFU Interview Coach ⭐⭐ *(the strongest moment — don't rush this)*

**SHOW:** **SIFU**. Click *Generate 5 Interview Questions* → point at the **"✨ AI-generated"** badge. Paste the weak answer, evaluate. Then paste the strong answer, evaluate.

> "This is SIFU — our interview coach. *Sifu* means master, or mentor.
>
> And this one is real AI. Not a script. I'll generate questions for a Frontend Engineer… these are being written live, right now.
>
> Now watch. I'll give it a deliberately lazy answer."

**PASTE:** `I would just use console.log and check what's wrong.`

> "…and SIFU calls it out. Look at the score, and it tells me exactly what's missing.
>
> Now a proper answer."

**PASTE:** `I'd profile with React DevTools first to find which component re-renders, then memoise with React.memo and useMemo. In my last project that cut render time by about 40 percent.`

> "Different score. Different feedback. And it checks my STAR structure.
>
> Nothing here was written in advance. That's a live Claude API call."

## 3:05 – 3:20 · Salary Insights

**SHOW:** **Salary Insights**.

> "Before you apply — know what you're worth. This shows where you sit against the Malaysian market, and which skills would actually move you up."

## 3:20 – 3:50 · Companies, Anti-Ghost, and Job Match

**SHOW:** **Companies** (point at the green/amber/red dots) → open a job → **View & Compare** (the 6-factor Match Score) → **Apply**.

> "And who to apply to. Every company carries an Anti-Ghost score. Green means they reply within days. Red means they go silent after interviews. Candidates deserve to know that *before* they invest their hope in an application.
>
> And when you open a job, you get a Match Score — six weighted factors explaining *why* you fit, what you're missing, and whether it's worth applying at all."

# PART 4 — MINISTRY

## 4:55 – 5:20

**SHOW:** **Ministry** → scroll to **Speed vs Stickiness**.

> "And nationally — the ministry.
>
> Right now MOHE measures how fast graduates get hired. Nobody measures whether they *stayed*.

## If you need to cut to 4 minutes

Trim in this order — never cut SIFU:
1. Salary Insights (0:15)
2. Story Video (shorten to a 5-second glance)
3. Career Path (mention the 4-year plan, skip the scenarios)
4. Shorten the opening to two sentences

## Recording tips

- **Use one-click login** — there's no password to fumble, which looks deliberate on camera.
- **SIFU takes 3–8 seconds to respond.** Either pause naturally ("while that's generating…") or cut the wait in editing.
- **Don't type long answers live.** Paste them — typing on camera is dead air.
- **Say the score out loud** when it appears. Judges watching on a phone may not read small numbers.
- **Record the whole thing in one take if you can**, then trim. Switching roles mid-take shows the platform is genuinely connected.
